# 🍴 Zomato Restaurant Data Analysis

## Alfido Tech Internship – Task 1

### 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the Zomato restaurant dataset to gain meaningful insights into restaurant ratings, customer preferences, pricing trends, and location-based patterns.

The analysis includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization techniques to better understand the factors influencing restaurant performance and customer satisfaction.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Perform data cleaning and preprocessing
* Analyze restaurant ratings and customer votes
* Identify popular cuisines and restaurant hotspots
* Explore the relationship between pricing and ratings
* Create insightful visualizations
* Generate business recommendations based on the analysis

---

## 📊 Dataset Information

**Dataset:** Zomato Restaurant Dataset

The dataset contains information such as:

* Restaurant Name
* Location
* Restaurant Type
* Cuisines
* Ratings
* Customer Votes
* Cost for Two People
* Online Ordering Availability
* Table Booking Availability

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

1. Most restaurants received ratings within the average to high range.
2. Certain locations showed a higher concentration of restaurants and customer activity.
3. Popular cuisines attracted greater customer engagement.
4. Restaurant pricing and ratings exhibited a relatively weak correlation.
5. Customer votes contributed significantly to restaurant visibility and popularity.

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Focus on high-performing restaurant locations for business expansion.
2. Promote popular cuisines through targeted campaigns.
3. Improve customer engagement through online ordering and digital services.
4. Leverage customer feedback to enhance service quality.
5. Utilize data-driven strategies for restaurant recommendations.
